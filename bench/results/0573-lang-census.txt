READINGS, each one a fact the 2026-09-02 brief on multilingual documents rests on:
- Half the records carry a language (6 663 of 13 287), spelled twelve ways for
  three languages: ISO codes in two cases, BCP-47 tags, English and French
  names, a bibliographic code (eng), and a two-language value "EN, VN" on 113
  records, which is the author marking a bilingual record by hand.
- Zotero's relation vocabulary has no translation predicate. dc:relation
  (Related Items, 32 here), dc:replaces (436) and owl:sameAs (1 889, the trail
  of duplicate merges) are all that exist.
- 888 records hold two or more PDFs; roughly 200 of them hold renderings in
  two languages, 157 of them English and Vietnamese. Under D6 (first-with-text)
  one rendering per record is indexed today and the other is skipped with a
  recorded reason, so on this library some 200 second-language bodies are
  outside the index by design.
- 562 same-language twins are the case D6 was written for (preprint and
  published version, scan and OCR copy) and stay as they are.

NOT MEASURED: pairs of separate records that are translations of one another
(same work filed twice, once per language). Nothing in the schema links them,
so a census needs the detector the brief proposes; this census cannot see them
and reports no number rather than a zero.
