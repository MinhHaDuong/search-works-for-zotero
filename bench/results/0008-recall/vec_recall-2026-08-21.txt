----------

          This is the ticket's acceptance criterion.

          Reported beside recall because recall alone cannot tell a real miss from a tie,

Verdict
-------
The pool that preserves the ranking is not a speedup, and the pool that is a speedup does
not preserve the ranking. Binary quantization is 13x faster than float32 AT k=30; asked for
the pool a rerank needs, it is not, because vec0's k-best structure costs more than linearly
in k. Ranking quality is nevertheless retained even where recall is not — sim kept is 0,994
at a pool of 4x, where recall is 0,628 — so the passages returned are near-substitutes, not
junk. Whether that is an acceptable trade is a decision about real embeddings, and these
fixtures only bracket them: `vec_quantize_binary` thresholds at zero, and a sentence
embedder's anisotropy would make the first pass worse on real data than on these zero-mean
fixtures unless the corpus mean is subtracted first.

The two-stage path therefore ships built, tested, and off by default (VEC_TWO_STAGE_DEFAULT).
